About

What I'm focused on

I've spent my career partnering with internal operational teams across industries to understand their workflows, identify inefficiencies, and design solutions that combine people, process improvements, and technology to make those teams run better.

Now I'm building deep fluency in applied AI, with a focus on how AI can be applied to the types of operational challenges I've spent years solving. I'm pursuing the IBM AI Product Management Professional Certificate and going well beyond the coursework by developing hands-on project case studies that demonstrate my approach: discovery, workflow mapping, solution design, and execution planning — all through the lens of AI-powered solutions.

Each project on this site documents that process from end to end.
